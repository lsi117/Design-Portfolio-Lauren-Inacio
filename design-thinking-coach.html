Facilitation: Enterprise Design Thinking Coach

Over a 12-week co-creator program, I coached a team of three non-designers and one new designer through Enterprise Design Thinking, from hopes and fears to a validated MVP.

12 weeksTeam of 4IBM Enterprise Design Thinking
Building trust

Starting with hopes and fears

At the start of the program I ran a hopes-and-fears activity to understand each person’s goals and worries: playbacks, time pressure, presenting, finding stakeholders. The team then wrote a social contract committing to learn EDT and empower one another.

The problem

A statement specific enough to act on, open enough to create

“How might we improve the virtual experience through effective communication for internal and external clients? So that we foster a fun and collaborative environment.”

Breaking the coursework into digestible pieces was how I addressed the team’s fear of being overwhelmed. I provided worked examples for problem statements and stakeholder definition, and the team mapped stakeholders across four IBM organizations to recruit as research participants.

Research to solution

Empathy maps, personas, and a prioritized big idea

The team built empathy maps from user research, developed their persona Adam, mapped the as-is scenario, and prioritized big ideas by feasibility and user importance. Their solution, a continuous learning plan using webinars and voice assistance, was validated through iterative research and staged as cupcake, birthday cake, and wedding cake versions.

Outcome
  • All four team members completed the program and earned their Enterprise Design Thinking: Co-creator certification.
  • The team delivered a validated to-be experience and a voice-command MVP concept.
  • I learned to coach by unblocking fears with examples, not lectures.
