Case study 02 · Self-initiated · Brand + product

A whole product, built from one written brief

Adventure Buddy connects people through real outings, not endless profiles. Create a hike, invite others, and let the trail do the talking. I owned every layer: palette, type, voice, iconography, mobile product, and marketing site, documented as a real design system.

In brief · the 60-second version
The problem Outdoor social apps index on profiles and matching rather than real outings.
The constraint Self-initiated and solo: no existing brand, assets, or team.
What I did Built the whole system from one written brief: palette, type, voice, iconography, mobile app kit, and marketing site.
The result A complete, documented design system with a live click-through demo.
The person

Maya, who is tired of performing

I wrote the product as a story before I designed a screen. Maya spends twenty minutes on onboarding, not because it is complicated, but because she keeps pausing to think. Hiking, obviously. Photography, absolutely. Kayaking, when the weather cooperates. No interest in anything that requires a rope and a helmet, at least not yet.

“Finally, it does not feel like performing.” It feels like filling out a form for her true self.

The idea

Real outings, not endless profiles

I deliberately avoided dating-app conventions, gamification, and the rugged extreme-sports look. No matching, no swiping, no streaks. People you hike with are buddies; a planned outing is an adventure; safety check-ins are trail check-ins.

The product

Every hike, at a glance

What it proves

Systems thinking, without a brief to hide behind

  • A token layer for colour, type, spacing, radii, shadow and motion, on a 4px grid.
  • A type pairing with a role for each family: literary serif for headlines, humanist grotesk for UI, mono reserved for trail stats.
  • Voice and tone guidance with a real vocabulary, including the words we refuse to use.
  • Two UI kits: the consumer mobile app and the marketing site that explains it.
  • Accessibility built in: visible focus rings, meaning never carried by colour alone, generous targets.

This portfolio is built in that system. You are looking at it.
