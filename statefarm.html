Design Thinking Workshop with State Farm & IBM

A multi-day co-creation workshop at the IBM Global Solution Center in Coppell, Texas. Four teams, each with a facilitator. I coached Team 1 from as-is scenario to a winning pitch.

IBM Global Solution CenterFacilitator, Team 1Winning team
The challenge

How might we optimize support for agent office connectivity — people, process, and technology — to minimize or eliminate impact to business?

Team 1 mapped the as-is scenario for Billy Bob, the State Farm insurance agent persona: too many touchpoints, inconsistent communication during recovery, misidentified tickets, and wrong priority levels.

From needs to idea

Agent Companion

After defining the needs statement (Billy Bob needs a way to access information and functionality when he needs it, so he can service customers quickly), the team generated and prioritized big ideas: vendor tracker, tech ratings, an information chatbot, remote equipment reboot. They combined them into one mobile app, Agent Companion, and I created its low-fidelity prototype.

Projected benefits
  • $1.78M in reduced follow-up calls annually
  • 60% increase in support productivity
  • 80% increase in agent satisfaction, 90% in customer satisfaction
  • Business continuity through wireless and cellular access
Outcome

Judged by peers on value proposition, business model, implementation, and innovation, Team 1 scored 106, the highest of the four teams, and won the workshop. Many attendees left planning to bring design thinking back to their own teams.
